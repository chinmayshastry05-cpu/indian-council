# Attribution

This project adapts the deliberation protocol of **Council of High Intelligence** by nyk (https://github.com/0xNyk/council-of-high-intelligence), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 nyk. The original license text is kept in `LICENSE`.

What comes from upstream (adapted, not copied verbatim): the multi-round structure (restate gate, blind Round 1, anonymized cross-examination, enforcement scan, final stance lines, weighted tally with a 1.5x domain seat and a 2/3 consensus rule, a Chairman who does not deliberate, kill criteria, one concrete next step, minority report, outcome ledger), the quick and duo modes, the triad and polarity-pair idea, and the agent-file layout.

What is new here: the roster (19 Indian thinkers, replacing the 18 upstream figures), the Chairman (Lord Krishna), every member's method, signature questions and blind spots (researched from primary texts and scholarship, sources listed in `docs/profiles.md` and in each agent file), the triads for this roster, and the validation script.

What was not ported: provider detection and model routing, `--models`, `--profile`, `.council.yaml`, session metadata and the diversity scorecard.

This project is an unofficial adaptation and is not affiliated with the upstream author. The council members are lenses written from texts; they are not the real people and do not speak for any tradition.
